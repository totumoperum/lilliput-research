Privacy

Privacy.

This is a provisional summary of the website's data handling and the principles relevant to archival enquiries.

Website

Visitors

The website is hosted as a static publication on GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, first-party analytics or advertising tracker. Hosting providers may retain technical access and security logs. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts.

Research files

Specific investigations may involve personal information about living people, including family relationships and references in public records. Use of that information needs a lawful basis, proportionate access and proper protection; case-specific arrangements depend on the enquiry.

Rights

Individuals may have rights to access, correct, object to, restrict or request erasure of personal data, subject to the relevant legal limits. If you received a letter, the return details are the current route for privacy questions.

Further details

Formal controller contact details, applicable legal bases, recipients, retention periods and international transfer arrangements need confirmation before a wider public launch. For guidance see the Irish Data Protection Commission.
