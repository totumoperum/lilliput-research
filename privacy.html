Privacy & personal data

Respecting the record — and the people in it.

This is a summary of website data and the principles relevant to archival investigations. It is not a substitute for a case-specific privacy notice.

Visitors to this website

The site is hosted as a static publication on GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, advertising tracker or first-party analytics. Hosting services may retain technical access or security logs. The typefaces are requested from Google Fonts, which means the browser may connect to Google to display them.

Genealogical investigations

Specific enquiries may involve personal information about living people, such as names, family relationships and references in probate documents, newspapers or civil records. A name match is not treated as proof of heirship. Processing of living-person information requires an appropriate lawful basis and proportionate use; case-specific disclosures and retention arrangements should be explained when applicable.

Requesting information or raising an objection

Individuals may have rights to access, correct, restrict, object to or request erasure of their information, subject to legal conditions and exceptions. If you received correspondence from Lilliput Research, use the return details and reference in that letter for privacy questions. A dedicated domain email is not yet operational.

For independent guidance see the Irish Data Protection Commission's transparency guidance.

Further information required before wider launch

This provisional summary needs to be supplemented with the confirmed identity and contact details of the data controller, applicable legal bases for processing, retention schedules, recipients and any international transfers. The information should reflect the practice's actual workflow.
