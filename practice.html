03 / Practice

The practice.

Lilliput Research is a small, independent practice founded and run by Rupert Murphy, an archaeologist and archival researcher. We work from Dublin 7, above the offices and bookshop of The Lilliput Press, with no corporate or legal affiliation to the publisher.

The founder

Rupert Murphy.

My background is in practical archaeology and archival research. Much of the work is patient: reading the original documents, buying the relevant probate records, checking names against dates and places, and reconstructing a family a generation at a time.

Sometimes the interesting part is how neatly the paper trail joins up. At other times there is a missing certificate or a contradiction that stops the research going any further. I keep those uncertainties visible. A bank, administrator or court—not Lilliput Research—determines legal entitlement.

Process

  1. Identify the assetInvestigate an unclaimed-estate notice, dormant asset or other public record.
  2. Establish the familyTrace the named individuals through wills, probate grants and civil registers to living branches where possible.
  3. Assemble the proofBuild a family tree with the original documents supporting each material relationship.
  4. Inform and assistContact a possible beneficiary where appropriate and establish what evidence the institution requires to examine a claim.

Professional boundaries

The family's claim, not ours.

Lilliput Research prepares genealogical evidence; it is not a bank, probate court or law firm. Any claim should be made by the person legally entitled or a representative they appoint. We do not ask to receive or hold recovered funds, and clients are welcome to use their own solicitor.

Possible relatives are under no obligation to engage us. They may approach the relevant institution independently. A family tree and supporting papers can help establish a relationship, but legal ownership, the identity of an account holder and the allocation of an asset must be determined by the appropriate institution or authority.
