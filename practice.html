03 / Practice

The practice.

Lilliput Research is an independent Ireland-based genealogical and archival research practice with an interest in difficult identity questions and overlooked documentary connections.

Approach

The paper trail, properly followed.

We work outward from original notices and attributable records. A family tree is a hypothesis until the links between its generations can be justified.

Research can involve archives across Ireland, Britain and continental Europe. Local spelling, family naming conventions and conflicting dates are treated as matters to resolve, not facts to smooth away.

Process

  1. Identify the sourceA published notice, archive entry or estate file provides the starting point.
  2. Test the identityMatch and challenge the relevant names, dates, places and relationships.
  3. Follow the familyReconstruct the related lines, keeping uncertainty plainly recorded.
  4. Prepare the evidenceGather the documents that a recipient, administrator or professional can independently assess.

Professional boundaries

Independence matters.

Lilliput Research documents historical and genealogical findings. It is not a solicitor's practice and does not determine legal heirship, release dormant assets or guarantee a successful claim.

Where appropriate, the relevant authority or a potential claimant's own solicitor should assess the legal position. Any proposed fee must be agreed in writing in advance; there is no advance payment to release an inheritance.
