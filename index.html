Our services

A family tree, with the records to support it.

We can prepare a family history setting out the relevant generations, with copies or references to certificates, wills, probate papers and historic records. Families may commission this work for their own interest, entirely apart from an inheritance enquiry.

Where the research identifies a possible heir to an overlooked asset, we can provide a clear account of the family connection and the available documentary evidence. The relevant bank, estate administrator or authority determines entitlement; recovered funds do not pass through us.
