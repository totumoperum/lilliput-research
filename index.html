LilliputResearch

Old accounts, forgotten relatives, and the paper trail between them.

I look through unclaimed-asset registers and old probate records. Sometimes an account leads me to a family I can trace through wills, births and marriages. The object is to put the documents together properly, not to guess who is owed what.

The work

Documents before conclusions.

Sometimes the curious part is how neatly the paper trail joins up. An old account leads to a probate record; a will names the children and grandchildren; a later will independently confirms the next generation. I spend time and money obtaining the original records rather than relying on a name that happens to match.
