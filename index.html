What we do

The family behind the name.

A brief newspaper notice, an old house register, a marriage recorded under another surname: taken separately, such details may appear unremarkable. Read together with wills, probate papers and parish or civil records, they can reveal the shape of a family over several generations, sometimes across several countries.

We reconstruct those relationships into a family tree supported by original documents and references. Where the evidence suggests a living relative may have an interest in an overlooked asset, we endeavour to inform them. The family history can be of interest in its own right; any legal entitlement remains for the relevant institution or administrator to determine.
