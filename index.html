LilliputResearch

Finding heirs. Reconstructing families. Recovering what has been overlooked.

Lilliput Research is an independent heir-research and genealogical practice based above The Lilliput Press, the book publishers, in Dublin. Founded and run by Rupert Murphy, an archaeologist and archival researcher, the practice identifies possible beneficiaries of unclaimed assets and assembles the family and probate records needed to support a claim.

The research

What we do

Connecting records to people.

Our work begins with an unclaimed-estate notice, dormant asset or other public record. We identify the individuals involved, reconstruct the family across generations and obtain the wills, probate papers and civil records that establish the relevant relationships.

Where research points to a living relative, we endeavour to make that person aware of a possible entitlement they may not have known about. The genealogical evidence is assembled for the claimant, their advisers and the institution responsible for assessing the claim.
