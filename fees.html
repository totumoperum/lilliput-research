Fees and independence

Fees.

Our introductory letter and a brief explanation of the possible family connection are free. Further research is optional and is undertaken only on terms agreed in writing.

Receiving a letter

There is nothing to pay for receiving a letter from us or asking why we wrote. Research undertaken before we made contact does not oblige you to engage Lilliput Research, and you may approach the relevant institution independently.

Commissioning research

If you wish to commission a family tree, further documentary research or assistance preparing genealogical evidence for a claim, we will explain the proposed scope, fee and any anticipated expenses in writing. A success-based fee, where proposed, requires a separate agreement. No percentage is charged merely because we have contacted you.

Any third-party certificates, translations or archive charges that would be payable separately must be agreed in advance. The website itself does not create a contract.

Recovered funds

Lilliput Research does not collect or distribute recovered funds. Any money should be paid directly to the claimant, estate or appropriately instructed solicitor or administrator. We do not require advance payments to release an inheritance.

Legal representation

We provide genealogical and documentary research, not legal advice. Families may instruct their own independent solicitor, and Lilliput Research does not determine ownership of an estate or account.
