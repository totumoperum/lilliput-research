Terms of engagement

Fees & independence.

The initial letter and brief explanation are free. If a family chooses to engage Lilliput Research to pursue an inheritance enquiry, our proposed success fee is 5% of funds actually recovered, subject to a written agreement.

A free initial letter

There is no fee for receiving a letter from us or for a short explanation of the possible family connection that prompted it. Research costs we have already incurred before approaching you do not oblige you to engage us. You may decline, consult your own solicitor or pursue the claim independently.

The proposed 5% commission

For an heir-tracing matter we initiate, our proposed standard success fee is 5% of the money actually recovered under an agreed engagement. The percentage is not charged merely because we have identified an asset or written to you. If nothing is recovered, there is no percentage-based success fee.

Before any work is commissioned, a written agreement must specify which recoveries it covers, when a fee becomes payable, treatment of tax and how any separately chargeable document, translation or third-party costs would be authorised. No such costs are imposed merely by receiving our letter. The terms are being reviewed with independent legal advice; this website is not itself an agreement.

Recovered funds

We do not require an advance payment to release an inheritance and do not receive or distribute client money. Funds should be paid directly to the claimant, their estate or an appropriately instructed solicitor or administrator.

Legal advice

We encourage recipients to instruct their own independent solicitor where needed. Lilliput Research undertakes documentary and genealogical research; it is not a law firm and does not determine ownership of any bank account or estate.
