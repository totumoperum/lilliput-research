Terms of engagement

Fees & independence.

An unexpected letter should never put somebody under an immediate obligation to sign an agreement or pay a fee.

Before an agreement

Research may already have been undertaken before we contact a possible beneficiary. There is no charge merely for receiving our letter or asking what prompted it. You are free to investigate independently and choose whether to engage us.

If you instruct us

Any proposal for further work should set out the services, the person responsible for payment, the proposed fee or success-fee percentage, how that fee is calculated, any applicable taxes, and whether documentary or third-party costs are additional. Such terms must be agreed in writing before work is commissioned.

There is no published universal fee percentage. Different matters and family circumstances may require different arrangements, and nothing on the website itself constitutes a contract.

Recovered funds

We do not require an advance payment to release an inheritance and do not receive or distribute client money. Funds should be paid directly to the claimant, their estate or an appropriately instructed solicitor or administrator.

Legal advice

We encourage recipients to instruct their own independent solicitor where needed. Lilliput Research undertakes documentary and genealogical research; it is not a law firm and does not determine ownership of any bank account or estate.
