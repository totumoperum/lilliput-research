Terms of engagement

Fees & independence.

The initial letter and brief explanation of our research are free, with no obligation to engage us. If you would like further research or help preparing documentation, we explain the scope and any fees in writing first.

A free initial letter

There is no fee for receiving a letter from us or for a short explanation of the possible family connection that prompted it. Research costs we have already incurred before approaching you do not oblige you to engage us. You may decline, consult your own solicitor or pursue the claim independently.

Further research

If you wish to commission more genealogical research, a documented family tree, or assistance preparing evidence for a possible claim, we will explain the proposed work and fees before you make a decision. Any success-based arrangement would be set out in a separate written agreement and would not arise merely from receiving our letter.

Any separately payable document, translation or third-party costs must be agreed in advance. Our engagement terms are being reviewed with independent legal advice; this website is not a contract.

Recovered funds

We do not require an advance payment to release an inheritance and do not receive or distribute client money. Funds should be paid directly to the claimant, their estate or an appropriately instructed solicitor or administrator.

Legal advice

We encourage recipients to instruct their own independent solicitor where needed. Lilliput Research undertakes documentary and genealogical research; it is not a law firm and does not determine ownership of any bank account or estate.
