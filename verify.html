Independent verification

Check a letter.

If you receive an unexpected enquiry about an inheritance, you should be able to check it without first handing over money, identification or bank details.

Before replying

Check who wrote to you

Lilliput Research is operated by Rupert Murphy in Dublin 7. Our website is lilliput-research.ie. We are independent from the book publisher whose premises we work above. You can write to the independently published postal address on our contact page, rather than relying only on contact details in the unexpected letter.

Ask about the evidence

Research can involve family trees, newspapers, household registers, archives, wills, public notices and locally discovered records. We should be able to explain the broad grounds for contacting you and provide suitable source references for independent checking without unnecessarily disclosing other people's information. If a formal claim notice has a deadline, verify it with the issuing institution.

Use your own solicitor

You do not need to use Lilliput Research to pursue a claim. If you prefer, show the letter to an independent solicitor, or ask the responsible bank or administrator directly about the appropriate claim process. We are not a legal practice and cannot guarantee any particular inheritance.

Do not send money or banking credentials

We do not require advance payments to release an inheritance, bank passwords, card details or access to accounts. Any request for sensitive identity documents should be verified and handled through a secure channel agreed with the appropriate institution or adviser.
