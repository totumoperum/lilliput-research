Before you reply

Confirm the sender

Lilliput Research is operated by Rupert Murphy in Dublin 7. Our website is lilliput-research.ie, and our postal address is published independently of any letter. The practice is separate from The Lilliput Press; the publisher does not handle our enquiries.

Ask what the enquiry concerns

You may ask for an explanation of the possible family connection and suitable references to the supporting records. Any stated deadline or official notice can be checked directly with the relevant institution. We will not publish or disclose other people's private details to establish our identity.

Choose your own adviser

You may show the letter to your own solicitor or contact the institution yourself. You are not required to instruct Lilliput Research, and we do not provide legal advice or guarantee the recovery of an asset.

Protect personal and banking details

We do not ask for an advance payment to release an inheritance, bank passwords, card numbers or access to accounts. If identity documents are necessary for a formal claim, their purpose and a secure submission method should be independently verified first.
