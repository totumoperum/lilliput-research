For solicitors, executors and administrators

Documentary genealogy.

When a family relationship is uncertain, or an estate has possible unidentified relatives, Lilliput Research can undertake a defined genealogical enquiry and provide a documented account for professional review.

Discuss an enquiry

Research instructions

A defined question. A referenced answer.

Enquiries may concern the descendants of a named individual, an uncertain parentage or marriage, a missing generation, an overseas branch, or the relationships relevant to probate and succession.

Depending on the instructions, we can supply a family-tree chart, relevant original records and an explanatory report identifying the established links, material gaps and unresolved identities. The scope and any necessary record costs are agreed in advance.

Research material available

  • Genealogical chart with source references for the relevant relationships
  • Copies or details of original records where access and reproduction are permitted
  • Chronology of relevant family events and probate proceedings
  • Report separating documented findings from provisional or unverified links
  • Identification of further certificates or archival records needed for review

Lilliput Research does not offer regulated legal advice, certify ownership, act as an estate administrator or receive client funds. Any sharing of living-person information must be appropriate to the agreed enquiry and applicable data-protection requirements.
