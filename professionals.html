For solicitors, executors and administrators

Documentary genealogy.

Where an estate has missing or unidentified relatives, we can undertake original-record research and family reconstruction to help establish the relevant lineage and identify further records to obtain.

Discuss an enquiry

Research work

From a name to a documented family.

We investigate civil and parish registers, wills, grants of probate, historic addresses, contemporary newspapers and archive holdings. The object is to produce research a professional can check—not simply an unsourced online family tree.

Instructions can be scoped to a particular identity problem, an overseas record or a branch of a family. We distinguish established links, provisional leads and documentary gaps.

Possible deliverables

  • A documented family-tree chart showing relevant generations and the underlying record references
  • Source references and copies of records where reproduction or disclosure is permitted
  • A chronology of births, marriages, deaths and probate events
  • Outstanding proof requirements, inconsistencies and rejected identity matches
  • A clearly scoped research summary suitable for professional review

We do not provide regulated legal advice, certify legal ownership, act as a court-appointed administrator or receive estate funds. Scope, fees, source-access restrictions and any permitted handling of personal data should be agreed before work begins.
